What kinds of graphics do you design?

We design across a wide range of formats, including logos and company branding, flyers and brochures, direct mail layouts, signage and displays, digital ads, email templates, presentation materials and multi-page documents. We can handle a single piece or a coordinated set of campaign visuals across channels.

How does design connect with print production?

Because design and print production happen under one roof with Allegra Portage, files are built to be press-ready from the start. That means fewer revision cycles and a smoother path from design approval to finished materials. The team that designs your piece understands exactly how it will be produced.

Can a design work across both print and digital formats?

Yes. We prepare designs for multiple output formats, so the same visual direction can work for a printed brochure, a digital ad and an email layout. Each format is adapted to its medium, keeping your brand consistent whether someone sees it on paper or on a screen.

Can you work with images I already have?

Yes. Whether you provide images that need editing or want a graphic created from scratch, our design team has the capabilities. Original concepts and edited graphics can both be applied to print or web.

Can design be built for ongoing or repeat use?

Yes. We create materials with reuse in mind, including templates for email, direct mail and campaigns that can be updated over time without a full redesign. This makes recurring programs more efficient and keeps your brand presentation consistent across every send.

Can you coordinate design across multiple channels?

Yes. We align visual design across print, digital and direct mail so your brand looks consistent wherever your audience encounters it. Whether you're running a single campaign or an ongoing multi-channel program, we keep the look and messaging organized from one place.

Do you outsource any of your work?

Never. We take pride in our work and we believe in accountability. Every line of code, every word of copy and every speck of imagery is created by us, for you. You can email, call or visit the individuals working on your project at any time.
