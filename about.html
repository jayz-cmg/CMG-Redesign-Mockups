How are Creative Marketing Group and Allegra connected?

Creative Marketing Group is the web, marketing and creative team that shares a building with Allegra Marketing Print Mail in Portage. We handle your website and digital marketing, Allegra handles print, mail and signs, and you deal with one local team, so your brand stays consistent from start to finish.

Do you outsource any of your work?

Never. We take pride in our work and we believe in accountability. Every line of code, every word of copy and every speck of imagery is created by us, for you. You can email, call or visit the individuals working on your project at any time.

What size companies do you work with?

We partner with organizations of all sizes, from local sole proprietors and small businesses to nonprofits, healthcare providers and multi-location companies. We tailor every project to your specific goals, budget and audience.

How do I get started?

Call us. The easiest way to get started is to reach out and have a conversation. We will ask a few questions about what you are trying to accomplish, who you are trying to reach and what your timeline looks like. From there, we put together a plan that fits your goals and gets your project moving.

Do I need a plan or a design ready before I call?

Not at all. Whether you have a rough sketch, a logo file or just a vision in mind, we will work with you to build something that represents your brand well and gets results.

Can you help with strategy, not just a website?

Yes. We start by understanding your goals, then recommend the right mix of web, search, email, print, direct mail and signage to help you reach more customers and grow your business. Think of us as your one-stop resource for anything with your logo on it.

Do you work with nonprofits?

Yes. We are especially dedicated to helping charities, schools, healthcare institutions, trade and business associations and other nonprofit organizations. If you represent a local nonprofit, contact us to see how we can help.

We make saying hello easy.

Whether you have a clear plan or a blank page, we are ready to help. Tell us about your project and we'll figure out the best path forward together.
