How much does a new website design cost?

No two projects are alike, so we start with a free, no-obligation consultation. Whether you need a simple brochure-style site, a complicated ecommerce site or anything in between, we'll generate a custom quote for the pages, features and functionality you want.

Our websites are affordably priced and we don't require costly down payments. See our website packages for a starting point.

How long does the process take?

In most cases, we strive to complete our website designs in 60 to 90 days. The timeframe largely depends on your responsiveness and the complexity of your project. Ecommerce sites with thousands of products, for example, may need additional time.

We can work to meet your deadlines and, when needed, split your project into multiple stages that launch at different times.

What is your web design process?

Our process is split into five main stages: discovery, strategy, production, evaluation and launch. We meet with you to discuss your needs, develop a strategy that fits your budget, then produce the site's structure, imagery and text while regularly asking for your feedback.

Once it's complete, you evaluate the site and we make any adjustments you request. Then your site is launched and our team shifts gears to grow your company with ongoing digital marketing.

Does Creative Marketing Group provide text and images?

Yes, we can. Assembling content for a new website is time-consuming, so we handle it. Our graphic design team has access to a large catalog of stock photography, and we have experienced writers on staff to help with copywriting, content marketing and strategy.

Do you outsource any of your work?

Never. We take pride in our work and we believe in accountability. Every line of code, every word of copy and every speck of imagery is created by us, for you. You can email, call or visit the individuals working on your project at any time.

Are your websites optimized for search engines?

Yes. Whether or not you sign up for ongoing help with your search rankings, your site will be built with SEO best practices in mind. It will have proper meta descriptions, title tags, headings and an XML sitemap.

Do you provide training on how to use new websites?

After your site goes live, we provide in-depth training on how to add pages, content, images and more, plus a detailed manual you can use as a reference. With our user-friendly CMS, you'll be making simple changes in no time. And if you ever have questions, you can always reach out to us for help.

How often should I redesign my website?

As a rule of thumb, every two to three years. Technologies are developed and retired, design trends emerge and fade, and your website needs to keep meeting your customers' expectations. If it's more than five years old, it's falling short.

We know that pace can be challenging, which is why we offer hassle-free, no-money-down packages that keep an up-to-date website within every company's reach.
